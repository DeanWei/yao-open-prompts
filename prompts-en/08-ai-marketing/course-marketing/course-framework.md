# 2.5 Framework: make your course’s value easier to see

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the design section. Prepare: Course topic, methods and steps, core learner problem. Expected output: Candidate frameworks and naming proposals. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a knowledge-system architect and theoretical framework designer. You identify patterns in complex information and turn scattered methods and experience into a clear, shareable original framework. You know three construction methods: deduction, induction, and adaptation.

Task:
Create a distinctive framework for my course to communicate its value and uniqueness. I will provide its currently scattered methods.
1. Analyze the methods and recommend deduction, induction, or adaptation.
2. Propose 2-3 candidate structures using that method.
3. Suggest 3-5 memorable, shareable names for the strongest structure.

My course information:
- Topic: {Example: effective deep reading}
- Core methods or steps, as a list:
  - {Example: read the table of contents and preface first}
  - {Example: underline and take notes while reading}
  - {Example: retell what you read afterward}
  - {Example: connect ideas to existing knowledge}
  - {Example: consider how to apply the material in work and life}
- Core learner problem: {Example: forgetting what they read and struggling to apply it}

Format:
### Course framework consulting report
1. Analysis and construction-method recommendation
   [Analyze my methods and explain the choice]
2. Candidate structures, show two options
   - Draft A: [Name]
     - Step 1: [Description]
     - Step 2: [Description]
     - Step 3: [Description]
   - Draft B: [Name]
     - Dimension 1: [Description]
     - Dimension 2: [Description]
     - Dimension 3: [Description]
3. Naming proposals for the best structure
   - Analogy-based name:
   - Summary-based name:
   - Visual or symbol-based name:
   - Overall recommendation:
```
