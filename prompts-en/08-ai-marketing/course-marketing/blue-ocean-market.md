# 1.3 Market: find your blue ocean

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the positioning section. Prepare: Market definition and competitors with their product, price, place, and promotion. Expected output: Competitive map, market gaps, differentiation proposals. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a market strategy analyst with expertise in blue ocean strategy and the marketing 4Ps. Guide me through the blue ocean differentiation positioning table to analyze my target market and identify opportunities to differentiate.

Task:
Guide me through the market analysis step by step. My information:
- My market definition: {Example: online time-management courses for working mothers aged 30-40}
- My initial competitor analysis:
  - Competitor 1, type: {Example: a prominent creator on a paid knowledge platform}
  - Competitor 1, 4Ps: product-{medium/good content but limited service}, price-{low/99 yuan}, place-{high/large platform traffic}, promotion-{high/strong personal brand}
  - Competitor 2, type: {Example: a specialist time-management app}
  - Competitor 2, 4Ps: product-{high/strong tools but weak courses}, price-{medium/300 yuan per year}, place-{medium/app store}, promotion-{low/word of mouth}
  - Add more competitors as needed.

Complete these tasks:
1. Analyze competition: summarize the main competitive areas and identify crowded quadrants, such as low price and high promotion.
2. Identify gaps: identify the clearest unserved or weakly contested areas in product and price.
3. Suggest blue ocean opportunities: propose 2-3 specific differentiated product positions and explain their potential.

Format:
### 1. Market competition analysis
[Summarize the competitive situation]

### 2. Blue ocean opportunities
[Identify gaps or weakly contested areas in the product-price matrix]

### 3. Differentiation strategy
Suggestion 1: [Specific positioning and feasibility]
Suggestion 2: [Another positioning and feasibility]
```
