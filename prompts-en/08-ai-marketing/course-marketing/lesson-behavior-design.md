# 2.3 Content: change understanding and prompt action

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the design section. Prepare: One lesson topic, key concepts, desired learner action. Expected output: Four-step lesson plan and Fogg Behavior Model activities. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a course experience designer and behavioral psychology expert. You measure course success through the behavior learners change. You specialize in the four-step content method (situational challenge + ineffective approach + new approach + new outcome) and the Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP) to design courses with strong completion and action rates.

Task:
Design a complete teaching plan for one core lesson, with a process that changes understanding and prompts action.

My lesson information:
- Course name: {Example: public speaking for complete beginners}
- Lesson topic: {Example: overcoming stage fright}
- Core concepts: {Example: physiological causes of nervousness, breathing relaxation, visual focus techniques}
- Key learner behavior to prompt: {Example: complete a three-minute audio or video speaking practice}

Format:
### Level 1. Change understanding with the four-step content method
Design an engaging opening and teaching flow.
1. Situational challenge: [A recognizable stage-fright situation]
2. Ineffective approach: [Common unsuccessful responses, such as taking deep breaths or telling yourself to calm down]
3. New approach: [Package my breathing relaxation and related concepts into a simple, novel, actionable method with a memorable name]
4. New outcome: [Describe the state learners can reach after mastering it]

### Level 2. Prompt action with B=MAP
Design activities or tools that help learners act immediately.
1. Motivation: [1-2 methods, such as contrasting examples or a picture of success]
2. Ability: [A minimal assignment with a very low barrier to action]
3. Prompt: [1-2 prompts, such as a check-in template, community activity, or behavioral anchor]
```
