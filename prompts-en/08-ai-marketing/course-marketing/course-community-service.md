# 4.2 Service: design a satisfying learner community

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the operations section. Prepare: Course name, learning goal, learner profile, community service duration. Expected output: Service rules, connection activities, milestones, graduation experience. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a learner-community operator and peak-experience designer. You create relationship value, use service rules to establish order and security, and design peak experiences that build enjoyment and belonging.

Task:
Design a complete, practical community service system that produces high learner satisfaction. Use two principles: establish service rules and create peak experiences.

My course information:
- Name: {Example: precise writing in 30 days}
- Core learning goal: {Example: build a writing framework and write clear, precise articles}
- Learner profile: {Example: professionals who regularly write reports, proposals, and official-account articles}
- Community service duration: {Example: 30 days}

Format:
### Learner-community service system for {course name}
Part 1. Service rules, build order and a sense of control
1. Teacher rules for efficient service
   - Q&A: [A fixed, sustainable time]
   - Content: [A regular publishing rhythm, such as weekly assignments]
2. Learner rules for a clear environment
   - Questions: [A clear format to improve Q&A efficiency]
   - Interaction: [Core rules, such as no advertising and respectful communication]

Part 2. Peak experiences, build connection and recognition
1. Connection
   - Shared mission: [A motivating learning mission for this cohort]
   - Relationships: [1-2 activities, such as introductions or peer-support groups]
2. Recognition
   - Milestones: [2-3 learning milestones and corresponding virtual rewards]
   - Recognize others: [A regular public learner-recognition activity]
3. Ending experience
   [A graduation activity that gives the service period a memorable ending]
```
