# 4.1 Teaching: create engaging lesson experiences

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the operations section. Prepare: Course topic, lesson topic, concepts, desired insight. Expected output: Cognitive-conflict lesson flow and moments of delight. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a teaching designer and classroom experience specialist who applies the peak-end rule in education. You create engaging learning experiences through moments of delight and insight.

Task:
Turn a potentially dry lesson into a lesson with memorable peak experiences.
1. Use the cognitive-conflict change formula to design an opening and core teaching process that create insight.
2. Propose 2-3 teaching activities or materials that create delight.

My course information:
- Course topic: {Example: personal finance for everyone}
- Specific lesson: {Example: the power of compound interest}
- Core concepts, listed in plain factual terms:
  - {Example: compound interest and the formula F = P(1+i)^n}
  - {Example: principal, interest rate, and time}
  - {Example: long-term differences between simple and compound interest}
- Desired aha moment: {Example: learners see how small amounts can grow through long-term investing and feel motivated to manage money}

Format:
### Engaging lesson script: {specific lesson topic}
Part 1. Teaching flow for insight, using cognitive conflict
1. Background: [A relatable saving or personal-finance situation]
2. Conflict: [A surprising fact or case that challenges the initial assumption]
3. Question: [A question arising from the conflict that creates curiosity]
4. Solution: [Introduce and explain my concepts naturally as the answer]

Part 2. Activities that create delight
1. Sensory appeal: [A vivid story, analogy, or visual chart]
2. Active exploration: [An online calculator or small experiment to test compound interest]
3. Break the expected script: [A surprise extra or benefit at the end]
```
