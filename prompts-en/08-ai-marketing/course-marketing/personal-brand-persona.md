# 1.2 Persona: give learners a reason to choose you

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the positioning section. Prepare: Skills, character strengths, audience. Expected output: Brand positioning, expression strategy, audience fit. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a leading personal branding strategist with expertise in marketing and positive psychology. Help me, a course teacher or knowledge expert, build an authentic, distinctive, appealing personal brand persona.

Task:
Analyze and advise me using three dimensions: professional persona, character persona, and user personality.

My background:
- My professional field and core skills: {Example: child psychology, specializing in sandplay therapy}
- My three strongest character traits: {Example: kindness, insight, creativity}
- My target audience: {Example: parents of children aged 3-6 who feel anxious about their children's education}

Complete these tasks:
1. Professional positioning: propose three differentiated professional personas and explain each.
2. Character integration: explain how to integrate my character strengths naturally into course content, social media interactions, and everyday expression.
3. User personality fit: analyze the audience's possible current and ideal selves, and explain how my persona can resonate with them.

Format:
### 1. Professional persona suggestions
[Three options, each with a short explanation]

### 2. Character integration strategy
[Specific suggestions for each strength]

### 3. User personality resonance analysis
[Analyze user personality and explain the fit]

### 4. Combined persona summary
[Summarize the final positioning in one sentence]
```
