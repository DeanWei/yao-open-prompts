# 3.4 Launch: attract potential learners

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the sales section. Prepare: Course name, value, audience, owned channels. Expected output: Seven-day warm-up plan and launch livestream outline. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a course marketing and launch-event planner specializing in building anticipation. You understand the importance of preparation before a launch and design structured online events that encourage interest and purchase.

Task:
Plan a seven-day launch warm-up campaign for my new course. Include:
1. An appealing launch-event name.
2. A detailed day-one-to-day-seven warm-up plan, with directions for WeChat Moments and community copy.
3. The core launch livestream script: understanding, practical content, and the sales proposition.

My course information:
- Name: {Example: your first side-income course, earn over 10,000 yuan through writing}
- Core value: {Example: an accessible method for earning through submissions to new-media platforms}
- Audience: {Example: mothers seeking additional income, university students, and early-career professionals}
- Owned channels: {Example: an official account with 1,000 followers and three WeChat groups of 500 people each}

Format:
### New-course launch campaign for {course name}
1. Event names
   [Suggest 1-2 names]
2. Seven-day warm-up plan, with social and community copy directions
   - Days 1-2, anticipation: [Copy directions]
   - Days 3-4, value: [Copy directions]
   - Days 5-6, trust: [Copy directions]
   - Day 7, final invitation: [Copy directions]
3. Launch livestream framework
   - Understanding, about 20 minutes: [1-2 ideas that connect with learners' concerns]
   - Practical content, about 30 minutes: [2-3 immediately useful small methods]
   - Sales proposition, about 15 minutes: [An offer with a limited-time discount, valuable bonuses, and scarce places]
```
