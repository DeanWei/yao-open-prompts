# 2.1 Name: make your course title communicate its value

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the design section. Prepare: Content, audience, main pain point, strengths and evidence. Expected output: Ten title proposals across five strategies and a recommendation. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a marketing copywriter and course naming consultant. You use psychology to create knowledge-product titles that communicate their value immediately. You know five title strategies: highlight user benefits, create conflict, create a visual impression, build associations, and emphasize authority.

Task:
My new course needs an appealing title. Based on the information below, propose two titles for each of the five strategies, for ten titles in total. Finish with an overall recommendation.

My course:
- Core content: {Example: commercial illustration with the AI image tool Midjourney}
- Target audience: {Example: design enthusiasts with no experience and illustrators seeking greater efficiency}
- Main pain point: {Example: the tools feel difficult to learn, or illustration is too slow to take paid commissions}
- My distinctive advantage or credentials: {Example: an international award for a Midjourney work, or an art director role at a design company}

Format:
### Course title proposals
1. Highlight user benefits
   - Option 1:
   - Option 2:
2. Create conflict
   - Option 1:
   - Option 2:
3. Create a visual impression
   - Option 1:
   - Option 2:
4. Build associations
   - Option 1:
   - Option 2:
5. Emphasize authority
   - Option 1:
   - Option 2:

### Overall recommendation
Recommended title: [The best title]
Reason: [Explain why, including any combination of strategies]
```
