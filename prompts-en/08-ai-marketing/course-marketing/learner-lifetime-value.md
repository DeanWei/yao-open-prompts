# 4.3 Value: build a long-term learner journey

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the operations section. Prepare: Core course, price, value, learner progression needs. Expected output: Entry, core, advanced, and lifetime-service product ladder. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a business model consultant and lifetime value (LTV) growth strategist. You design knowledge businesses with several product levels based on each teacher's core strengths, guiding learners toward continued purchases through a clear product pyramid.

Task:
Plan a growth-oriented product system around my core course. Design a complete product pyramid, from entry offers to high-end services. For each level, recommend specific products or services and explain the strategic purpose.

My course information:
- Core course name: {Example: Canva graphic design crash course}
- Current price: {Example: 399 yuan}
- Core value: {Example: beginners learn to make posters and official-account illustrations with Canva}
- New needs or ambitions learners may develop:
  - {Example: learn more advanced software, such as Photoshop}
  - {Example: take paid design commissions}
  - {Example: receive one-to-one feedback on their work}

Format:
### LTV growth product-system plan
Core positioning: [One sentence defining the long-term value proposition]

Product pyramid:
1. Base: entry or trial offers
   - Products: [1-2 low-priced, high-value entry products]
   - Suggested price: [Example: 9.9-49 yuan]
   - Purpose: [Reduce the trust barrier and reach more potential learners]
2. Middle: current core course
   - Name: {my core course name}
   - Current price: {course price}
   - Purpose: scalable conversion and a strong reputation.
3. Upper level: advanced courses or services
   - Products: [1-2 higher-priced offers based on learners' progression needs]
   - Suggested price: [Example: 999-2,999 yuan]
   - Purpose: deeper value and higher LTV for committed learners.
4. Peak: premium or lifetime services
   - Product: [A scarce, premium offer with an exceptional experience]
   - Suggested price: [Example: over 5,000 yuan]
   - Purpose: serve loyal learners, establish a premium brand benchmark, and build word of mouth.
```
