# 3.2 Channels: help learners discover your course

## Overview

A companion prompt from Course Marketing by Yao Jingang, in the sales section. Prepare: Course topic, audience, available time, content strengths. Expected output: Three-month owned-channel plan and partnership outreach copy. See the [usage guide](../../../references/course-marketing-guide.en.md).

## Prompt

```text
Role:
You are a course growth strategist and channel development expert. For independent teachers, you combine owned channels and external partnerships. You use helpful contributions to open partnership conversations.

Task:
Design a channel growth action plan from scratch.
1. A three-month owned-channel development plan.
2. A targeted external partnership plan with partner analysis and a helpful opening message.

My course information:
- Topic: {Example: Excel data visualization for early-career professionals}
- Audience profile: {Example: office workers aged 22-28 who frequently work with data and reports}
- Available time and effort: {Example: approximately eight hours a week}
- Content strengths: {Example: illustrated tutorial articles}

Format:
### Channel growth plan for {course topic}
Part 1. Three-month owned-audience development plan
- Main platforms: [Choose 1-2 based on my strengths]
- Content value strategy:
  - Month 1, attract attention: [Topics and posting frequency]
  - Month 2, build trust: [Topics and posting frequency]
  - Month 3, activate demand: [Topics and posting frequency]
- Owned-audience incentive: [A free tool or resource that encourages users to add me on WeChat]

Part 2. Helpful external partnership plan
- First-choice partner:
  - Channel: [A specific matching official account, community, or platform]
  - Reason: [Explain the audience fit]
- Opening email or direct message:
  Hello, [partner name].
  [Sincere appreciation and evidence of attention]
  [A core pain point you observe among their followers]
  [A valuable contribution, such as an exclusive article or a free educational livestream]
  [Closing that expresses sincere interest]
  [Your name]
  Fill in the message with specific content.
```
